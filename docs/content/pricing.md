# Pricing

The Hauska MCP Server catalog is **free to start** and stays free for
non-commercial and small-scale commercial use. Paid tiers raise the rate
limits for production and embedder use.

> **Pricing is in finalization.** The price points below are placeholders.
> Contact [hello@hauska.dev](mailto:hello@hauska.dev) for early access
> and current pricing.

| Tier | Daily limit | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | 1K / day / IP, 10K / day / key | $0 |
| **Developer Pro** | 50,000 / day | In finalization |
| **Team** | 500,000 / day | In finalization |
| **Embedder License** | Unmetered | Per agreement |

## What every tier includes

- The full public catalog tool surface.
- A source citation (DID, content hash, source document) on every result.
- The provenance envelope: no answer without a traceable atom.

## What the paid tiers add

- Higher per-minute and per-day rate limits (see [Tiers](tiers.html)).
- A key-scoped quota instead of a shared per-IP allowance.
- The Embedder License additionally removes the response attribution
  requirement (see [Attribution](attribution.html)).

## Free is a real tier, not a trial

The free catalog is not a time-limited trial. It is the Layer 1 substrate,
free by design, because broad agent access is the point. Paid tiers exist
for callers whose volume or commercial scale needs them.
